Motor enable jumpers mean fixed-speed drive: the stick controls direction and steering, not speed. Auto drives forward, then scans the 0–180° field in 15° steps when an obstacle is within the configured 750 mm clearance threshold. Turn timing is open-loop and must be calibrated for the chassis.

Robot features Tap to toggle

Auto turret pilot pauses driving so you can aim the turret; turn it off to resume automatic obstacle scanning. The laser automatically shuts off after 5 seconds and on disconnect. RC obstacle pan requires the RC pan toggle and a working ToF sensor.

Monophonic MIDI Upload to robot RAM

Choose a Standard MIDI file.

Standard MIDI format 0/1 only. One track is converted to a single-note melody; overlapping notes use the highest pitch. Up to 256 events; pitches are capped at 4 kHz. The tune is held in RAM until reset. Uploading replaces the built-in Panzerlied for playback; upload again after reboot. The buzzer cannot play chords or full-quality audio.
